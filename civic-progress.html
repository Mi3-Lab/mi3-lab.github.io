SAE / GM CivicProgress Challenge

Accessible mobility through connected and autonomous vehicle technology

The Mi3 Lab is proud to participate in the SAE / GM CivicProgress Challenge, an initiative organized by SAE International and General Motors that engages university teams in developing connected and autonomous vehicle technologies with clear civic benefit. Our lab's project focuses on accessible mobility—bringing intelligent transportation systems to populations that are too often left out of the autonomy conversation, including riders with mobility, sensory, or communication differences.

The effort spans a lab research team, two senior capstone design teams, and a summer research intern, with contributions across perception, planning, human–machine interaction, and system integration.
